# Category H — Preference Optimization and RL Post-training — Discovery & Ranking

Methodology note (corrected re-run): For each topic, free/public candidates were actually fetched (WebFetch) before inclusion, not scored from search snippets alone, except where fetching genuinely failed or the search budget was exhausted mid-session (each such row is flagged "(provisional – snippet only)"). Genuinely on-topic resources that turned out to be paywalled or blocked (Medium/Towards Data Science, Substack "Paid" posts, etc.) were kept in the ranked list at their true relevance position rather than dropped — these carry Score = "None (blocked/paywalled - not scraped)" and were not scraped. Candidates that were fetched and found to be shallow/SEO-listicle-style were either excluded in favor of deeper alternatives or kept with an honestly low Depth/Pedagogy-driven score rather than silently dropped.

Scoring formula for fetched FREE/PUBLIC items: Score = 0.25·Depth + 0.20·Relevance + 0.20·Pedagogy + 0.10·Implementation + 0.10·Authority + 0.10·Currency + 0.05·UsefulnessEvidence. UsefulnessEvidence (independent citation/discussion signal) was generally not knowable from search/fetch alone; where marked "unknown" the remaining criteria were renormalized to 100% and the result flagged "(provisional – usefulness unknown)".
